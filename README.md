# Ecommerce-Dashboard
EShop Sales Analysis Dashboard
Project Overview
This project is an interactive Excel dashboard for analyzing EShop sales performance. It summarizes orders, revenue, customer spending, and delivery time, with charts that help explore patterns across occasions, product categories, hours, months, products, and cities.
Dashboard KPIs
- Total Orders: 95
- Total Revenue: ₹35,20,984
- Average Customer Spending: ₹3,302.98
- Order Delivery Time: 5.5
Dashboard Analysis
The dashboard includes:
- Revenue by Occasion — compares sales across occasions such as Anniversary, Birthday, Diwali, Holi, Raksha Bandhan, and Valentine’s Day.
- Revenue by Category — shows revenue across categories including Cake, Colors, Mugs, Plants, Raksha Bandhan, Soft Toys, and Sweets.
- Revenue by Hour — explores how revenue changes throughout the day.
- Revenue by Month — highlights monthly sales patterns and seasonal peaks.
- Top 5 Products by Revenue — ranks products by revenue.
- Top 10 Cities by Orders — compares order counts across leading cities.
Interactive Filters
Users can explore the dashboard with filters for:
- Order date
- Delivery date
- Occasion
Charts and KPI values can change based on the selected filters.
Key Takeaways
- The dashboard makes it easy to compare sales performance across occasions and product categories.
- Monthly and hourly views help identify periods of higher and lower revenue.
- Product and city rankings can support decisions about promotions, inventory, and market focus.
- The displayed results depend on the filters selected in the dashboard.
Tools
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and charts
- Interactive date filters and slicers
Purpose
This dashboard is designed to turn sales data into a clear, interactive summary that supports business analysis and decision-making.
